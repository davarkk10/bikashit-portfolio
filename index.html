Case study 04 · Real public data · SQL + Power BILive Power BI report

Late deliveries cost stars

PostgreSQL analysis and a 6-page Power BI report on 96,478 real delivered orders from a Brazilian marketplace: how much does late delivery hurt reviews, and where should logistics look first?

2.27 vs 4.29avg review, late vs on time
72%of late orders: seller shipped on time
17.7%of late orders on one lane (SP → RJ)
Decision
Fix the São Paulo → Rio lane and peak-month carrier capacity first; coach the 63 sellers behind 15.6% of late orders.
Proof
Data checks first, then 10 saved queries (CTEs, window functions) with CSV results. The live report runs on the same SQL views, and its 12 headline numbers reconcile to the query results.
Boundary
Real but historical data (Brazil, 2016–2018). Shows correlation, not cause; the dataset has no carrier field.
